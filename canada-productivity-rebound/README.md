## Important research boundary
The chart data are transcribed from the cited official Statistics Canada and BLS releases. The complete historical ranking since 1981, contribution-weighted industry decomposition, and a harmonized Canada–US long-run comparison have not been calculated. The article explicitly discloses this and does not claim those analyses are complete. Do not remove that disclosure unless those tests are performed and documented.
